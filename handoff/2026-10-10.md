## [08:26] [claude] Nebula: drawn smoothly on the star canvas instead of a snapping CSS animation
- **Changed:** public/js/main.js, public/css/shared.css, public/css/zephyy.css, AGENTS.md, BLUEPRINT.md
- **What/why:** Doshus: "lets optimize that cosmic-bg". `.cosmic-bg` animated `background` through 20 gradient keyframes over 40s; gradients don't interpolate, so it snapped every 2s and repainted the whole viewport each time. The keyframes are now data in main.js (`NEBULA`, 20 "token alpha where" moments) and a second WebGL program draws them as a full-screen pass before the stars: the browser's linear-gradient math, stops mixed in premultiplied oklab over black, dithered, moments interpolated in time, one turn per 40s. The oklch→sRGB math moved from JS into one GLSL function both programs share (star tints and glow now go up as oklab; the JS helper is `labOf`, since the lint flags `oklab(`). `.cosmic-bg` is the first moment, still (first paint, no-WebGL fallback), and main.js hides it while the canvas covers it (unhidden on context loss). Zephyy's pages keep their own layered background via `--sky-nebula: none` on #stars (zephyy.css). The 22-line keyframes block is gone.
- **Verified:** Chromium + SwiftShader via Playwright. Fidelity vs the old CSS frozen at 0s and at 14s (261deg, pink phase): median colors over 10 patches differ by at most 9/255 and 8/255 (a gamma-sRGB first draft was off by 14 near black; oklab fixed it); side-by-side eyeballed. Smoothness: one patch sampled every 500ms for 4s, old 27→21→21→21→14→14→14→14→23 (holds, then jumps), new 24 19 16 14 16 21 23 23 22. Cost: 6s idle trace on index.html 3 paints / 0ms raster, was 9 full-width paints / 87ms. Zephyy page shows her teal/violet layers, `--sky-nebula` reads none, backdrop never hidden; reduced motion: still frame with the nebula; WEBGL_lose_context lose/restore: backdrop shown while lost, hidden again after, nebula redrawn, no errors; no WebGL: no canvas, backdrop visible. `bun run check` PASS.
- **NOT verified:** a real GPU (on SwiftShader, where the GPU is the CPU, idle fps went 17.8-19.6 → 16.0-16.4: the full-screen pass costs software rendering; on a GPU it is a few dozen ALU ops a pixel); Firefox; phones; angles other than 135 and 261deg against the CSS math; a 40s wraparound watched live (the moment index wraps by `% moments.length`).
