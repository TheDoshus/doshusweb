Sky lab

Same sky, different engines. Switch below and watch the meter.

WebGL

Every star is one point in a single draw call per frame. Each one twinkles on its own clock, drifts smoothly between pixels, and shifts with your pointer and your scroll. No DOM work per frame.

Layers

The same stars as box-shadow dots, three depths, slid by CSS animations: zero JavaScript per frame. Trade-offs: stars twinkle in groups, no scroll parallax, and every layer is a texture about two screens wide.

Current

What ships today: 245 elements, each one moved by JavaScript every frame, each its own composited layer.

Judging it

Give each engine ten seconds. late is the share of frames that took 1.5× longer than usual: that's the hitch you feel. Try it with the cards off too, since their blur re-renders whenever the sky moves behind them.
